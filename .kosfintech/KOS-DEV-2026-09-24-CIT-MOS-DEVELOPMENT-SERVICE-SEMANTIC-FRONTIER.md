## 1. Purpose

This artifact establishes the first evidence-based semantic frontier for the Development Service within CIT_MOS.

It records independently existing KOSFintech development-control evidence that supports the proposed CIT_MOS controlled technology-development service concept.

It does not establish production entities, authorization, permissions, workflows, or constitutional authority.

## 2. Source Evidence

The principal source evidence is:

- docs/DEVELOPMENT_WORKABILITY_CHARTER.md
- .kosfintech/changes/README.md
- .kosfintech/changes/KOS-DEV-0003.md
- subsequent verified KOS-DEV development-baseline records.

The Development Workability Charter establishes a non-negotiable development cycle:

    INSPECT
       ↓
    UNDERSTAND
       ↓
    PLAN
       ↓
    CHANGE
       ↓
    TEST
       ↓
    RECORD
       ↓
    HANDOFF

The Charter also requires consequential development changes to have traceable identity and requires the contributor to preserve a trace from source requirement through implementation, tests, result, Git commit, and handoff.

## 3. Evidence-Based Development-Service Requirement

The existing evidence supports the following developmental requirement:

> CIT_MOS may provide a controlled technology-development service environment through which consequential technology-development work can proceed from identified requirement through inspected change, verified implementation, recorded evidence, Git-provenanced result, and contributor handoff.

This is an evidence-derived developmental requirement.

It is not a claim that CIT_MOS production capability already exists.

## 5. Development Service Boundary

The evidence supports a distinction between:

    Development Service
    Development Method
    Development Participant
    Development Artifact
    Development Evidence
    Development Authority

These concepts SHALL NOT be treated as automatically equivalent.

The development-control method is evidence supporting the service concept; it is not itself the complete CIT_MOS service domain model.

## 6. Relationship to Software Development Engineering

Software Development Engineering remains the first intended CIT_MOS service pipeline.

The evidence in this frontier strengthens the basis for that selection because the KOSFintech development-control lineage demonstrates an established controlled software-development operating process.

However:

    Development-control process != complete CIT_MOS service model
    Software Development Engineering != DSL
    CIT_MOS != DSL

## 7. Relationship to DSL-Oriented Development Engineering

DSL-Oriented Development Engineering remains an initial specialized capability within the Software Development Engineering pipeline.

The DSL remains separately bounded.

CIT_MOS may provide services supporting DSL specification, correspondence, validation, maturation, synchronization, or onboarding where independently established.

Such service provision does not establish CIT_MOS ownership of DSL semantics.
