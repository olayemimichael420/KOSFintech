# CIT_MOS Domain Declaration

**Domain Identifier:** CIT_MOS
**Domain Name:** Computer and Information Technology Management Operating System
**Domain Version:** v0.1.0
**Status:** DEVELOPMENTAL DOMAIN DECLARATION
**Provisioning Contract:** DAPC v0.1
**Production Dependency:** NONE
**Authority Effect:** NONE

## 1. Domain Purpose

CIT_MOS is intended to provide an independently bounded digital operating foundation for computer and information technology management and related technology-service operations.

The domain is intended to support independently scoped technology-service environments, software development engineering, technology operations, coordination, administration, service delivery, and related operational activities where independently established.

The declaration does not yet establish the detailed operational semantics of CIT_MOS.

## 2. Domain Boundary

CIT_MOS concerns computer and information technology management operations.

The domain boundary may include, where independently established:

- technology-service environments;
- technology-service tenants;
- software development engineering;
- technology operations;
- development and engineering service pipelines;
- technical projects and work units;
- service coordination;
- engineering resources and assignments;
- operational records and reporting;
- technology-service delivery;
- DSL-oriented development engineering;
- DSL maturation and onboarding services.

These are candidate areas only and SHALL NOT become implemented capabilities without independent domain validation.

## 3. Intended Application Surface

The eventual CIT_MOS application may provide facilities for:

- technology-service tenant administration;
- software development engineering coordination;
- technology-service pipeline coordination;
- engineering work coordination;
- project and work-unit management;
- technical service delivery;
- engineering records;
- operational reporting;
- controlled communication;
- DSL-oriented development engineering services;
- DSL maturation and onboarding support.

The actual capability frontier remains to be established.

## 4. Known Actors

Potential actors include:

- CIT_MOS platform operators;
- technology-service tenants;
- tenant administrators;
- software development engineering participants;
- engineering service units;
- designated engineering officials;
- project/work coordinators;
- technical contributors;

Actor identity, capacity, membership, responsibility, authority, service relationship, and application permissions SHALL remain distinct concepts.

## 5. Explicit Domain Independence

CIT_MOS SHALL NOT be defined as:

- SMOS adapted for technology operations;
- CMOS adapted for technology operations;
- CDA_MOS adapted for technology operations;
- a generic combination of existing MOS domains;
- an inheritance layer over SMOS;
- an inheritance layer over CMOS;
- an inheritance layer over CDA_MOS;
- the DSL itself;
- a semantic parent of the DSL;
- a parent domain over other MOS domains.

Existing MOS domains may be consulted only as engineering evidence where relevant.

## 6. Explicit Platform and Ownership Boundary

KOSFintech is not a CIT_MOS tenant.

KOSFintech provides the platform, ownership, and operational environment under which CIT_MOS may be established as an independently bounded MOS domain.

CIT_MOS may provide independently scoped technology-service tenancy without converting KOSFintech into a tenant of CIT_MOS.

Ownership, platform relationship, tenant relationship, service relationship, semantic identity, operational authority, and application permissions SHALL remain distinct concepts.

## 7. Initial Service Pipeline Boundary

Software Development Engineering is identified as one intended CIT_MOS service pipeline.

DSL-Oriented Development Engineering is identified as an initial privileged operational service capability within that pipeline/service environment.

“Privileged” in this declaration means an initial or primary operational position within CIT_MOS. It SHALL NOT be interpreted as constitutional authority, semantic ownership of CIT_MOS, semantic ownership of the DSL, or authority over other MOS domains or their tenants.

The DSL remains a separately bounded developmental resource and SHALL NOT be redefined as CIT_MOS merely because CIT_MOS provides services for its maturation or onboarding.
