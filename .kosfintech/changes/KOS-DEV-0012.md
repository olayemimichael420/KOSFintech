## 1. Purpose

This artifact establishes the unified minimum semantic foundation for CIT_MOS (Computer and Information Technology Management Operating System).

Its purpose is to define the minimum meaning, boundaries, relationships, distinctions, evidence requirements, and adoption progression necessary for CIT_MOS to exist as a coherent independently bounded technology-management domain.

This contract consolidates the developmental evidence established through the CIT_MOS domain declaration, development-service semantic frontier, platform/tenant boundary frontier, technology-service architectural direction, evidence closure, semantic pause, KOS-DEV-0007 through KOS-DEV-0011, and the KOSFintech-wide operational-purpose evidence.

This contract is deliberately extensible. It does not attempt to define every present or future CIT_MOS service, activity, organizational structure, office, officer, responsibility, assignment, delegation, workflow, or technical implementation.

**Extensibility != Undefined Scope.**

Every future semantic extension requires independently identifiable requirement evidence, bounded semantic meaning, applicable responsibility and relationship rules, verification evidence, provenance, and the relevant authorization gate.

## 2. Domain Identity

CIT_MOS is an independently bounded Management Operating System concerned with computer and information technology management and related technology-service operations.

CIT_MOS may support technology-service environments, technology-development services, technology operations, coordination, administration, service delivery, engineering work, service-value activities, evidence, continuity, and other technology-management functions where independently established.

CIT_MOS is not:

- KOSFintech itself;
- a generic platform permission layer;
- a replacement for constitutional or institutional authority;
- DSL itself;
- the semantic parent of DSL;
- the semantic parent of SMOS, CMOS, CDA_MOS, or other independently bounded MOS domains.

KOSFintech may provide the platform, ownership context, operational environment, and engineering environment within which CIT_MOS is developed or operated.

Platform relationship does not itself establish CIT_MOS semantic meaning, tenant identity, service authority, institutional authority, or constitutional authority.

## 5. Service and Service-Value Architecture

CIT_MOS shall distinguish the following concepts where applicable:

- Service;
- Service Provider;
- Service Recipient;
- Service Pipeline;
- Service-Value Action;
- Activity;
- Outcome;
- Evidence;
- Verification;
- Value;
- Completion;
- Continuity.

A **Service** is a bounded technology-management or technology-service provision having an identifiable purpose, applicable participants, service relationship, relevant activities or actions, expected result or outcome, responsibility context, and evidence sufficient to distinguish the service from unrelated platform capability.

A **Service Provider** is an independently established participant or organizational structure providing an applicable service.

A **Service Recipient** is an independently established participant, organization, environment, or other recipient receiving or organizing an applicable CIT_MOS service.

A service relationship does not automatically create tenancy, ownership, organizational subordination, or authority.

A **Service Pipeline** is an identifiable pathway through which an applicable service proceeds through relevant stages, activities, evidence, verification, completion, and continuity.

A **Service-Value Action** is an identifiable action performed within an established service context that contributes to the intended service purpose or result.

An **Activity** is an identifiable unit of technology-management work, participation, coordination, operation, development, administration, verification, documentation, maintenance, handoff, or other independently established work.

These concepts remain distinct even when represented together by software.

## 6. Value, Contribution, Evidence and Verification

CIT_MOS shall preserve the distinction between activity, evidence, verification, recognition, outcome, value, and human worth.

The following distinctions are mandatory:

Recorded != Verified.

Verified != Valuable.

Valuable != Monetary.

Monetary Value != Human Worth.

Recording an activity does not establish its value.

Evidence supports a claim but does not automatically establish authority.

Verification establishes the result of an applicable verification process but does not automatically establish constitutional, institutional, or legal authority.

Economic representation is an operational construct and shall not be treated as a measure of human dignity or human worth.

Where CIT_MOS represents technology-service activity or value, the relevant evidence, verification, context, responsibility, and applicable rules shall remain distinguishable.

## 7. Participant and Human Agency Boundary

A Participant is a person, organization, service unit, environment, or other independently established actor participating in an applicable CIT_MOS service or activity.

Participant identity, membership, capacity, responsibility, assignment, permission, and authority shall remain distinct.

Participant != Authority.

Participant != Office.

Participant != Officer.

Participant != Tenant.

Software may record, organize, calculate, notify, compare, apply defined rules, expose evidence, and support human decisions.

Technical capability shall not be treated as institutional authority.

Higher-order decision authority must be established independently of software.

A participant shall not be reduced to a score, role, transaction, membership number, attendance record, economic value, or algorithmic classification.

## 8. Organizational Structure

CIT_MOS may support organizational structures required for independently established technology-management services.

The minimum conceptual vocabulary includes:

- Organization;
- Office;
- Officer;
- Function;
- Responsibility;
- Duty;
- Assignment;
- Delegation;
- Oversight;
- Accountability.

An **Office** is an established organizational position or functional locus having a defined purpose.

An **Officer** is an independently designated actor occupying or performing an established office or function.

A **Function** identifies an established organizational purpose or operational role.

A **Responsibility** identifies an accountable duty or function.

A **Duty** identifies an applicable obligation or required function within an established responsibility context.

An **Assignment** identifies allocation of an established responsibility, duty, activity, or work to an applicable participant.

A **Delegation** identifies an independently established transfer or authorization of a responsibility or function within an applicable authority structure.

**Office != Officer.**

**Officer != Participant.**

**Responsibility != Assignment.**

**Assignment != Delegation.**

**Delegation != Automatic Authority.**

No database record, application role, office record, officer record, assignment, or delegation record shall manufacture constitutional or institutional authority merely through technical representation.

## 9. Oversight and Accountability

Where CIT_MOS services affect people, institutions, records, resources, value, or consequential technology operations, appropriate responsibility and traceability shall be distinguishable.

Oversight shall remain distinct from execution.

Accountability shall remain distinct from technical attribution.

Technical attribution identifies who or what performed or caused an operation within the technical system; it does not by itself establish institutional ownership, office, appointment, constitutional authority, or legal responsibility.

Technical Attribution != Institutional Ownership.

Audit Record != Constitutional Proof.

Successful Workflow != Authorization.

## 10. Tenant and Service-Recipient Boundary

CIT_MOS may support independently scoped technology-service environments or tenants where independent requirements establish the tenant concept.

A Tenant is not established merely by:

- a database record;
- a database table;
- a service relationship;
- an isolation mechanism;
- an administrator role;
- an application permission;
- ownership of infrastructure;
- provision of technology services.

A Service Recipient may be a Tenant where independently established requirements make that relationship valid.

Service receipt does not automatically create tenancy.

Therefore:

Service Recipient != Tenant by default.

Tenant != Service Provider.

Tenant != Platform Owner.

Platform != Domain.

Domain != Tenant.

Owner/Operator != Tenant.

KOSFintech shall not be represented as a CIT_MOS tenant merely because it owns, operates, hosts, or provides the environment for CIT_MOS.

Tenant identity, admission, ownership, lifecycle, scope, isolation, administration, data boundaries, and service relationships remain subject to further semantic validation.

No CIT_MOS tenant schema, repository, service, administrator role, or lifecycle implementation is authorized by this contract.

## 15. Lifecycle and State

A CIT_MOS service, activity, action, assignment, or responsibility may require lifecycle states where independently established.

Lifecycle states shall be defined according to the meaning of the particular concept rather than imported automatically from an analogous system.

A service lifecycle may require distinctions such as initiation, establishment, active execution, evidence or verification, completion, cancellation or termination, and continuity, where relevant.

No universal lifecycle taxonomy shall be inferred before service-specific requirements justify it.

A successful workflow does not automatically establish authorization.

Completion does not automatically establish constitutional finality, institutional acceptance, economic value, or social impact.

## 16. Completion, Outcome and Impact

Service completion shall mean satisfaction of the applicable validated completion conditions for that particular service.

Completion != Verification in all cases.

Completion != Authorization.

Completion != Constitutional Finality.

Completion != Human Worth.

Completion != Social Impact.

An outcome is the relevant result produced by an applicable service or activity.

Impact is a further real-world consequence requiring appropriate evidence.

Implementation, deployment, or successful testing shall not automatically satisfy proof obligations concerning adoption, reliability, verification quality, institutional usefulness, user benefit, inclusion, opportunity, service exchange, institutional continuity, or measurable real-world impact.
