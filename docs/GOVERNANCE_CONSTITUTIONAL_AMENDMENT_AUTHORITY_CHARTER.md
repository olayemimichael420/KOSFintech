# Governance Constitutional Amendment Authority Charter

**Status:** PROPOSED — DEVELOPMENT CONTROL ARTIFACT
**Constitutional authority:** NOT CREATED BY THIS DOCUMENT
**Amendment power:** NOT GRANTED
**Amendment authority:** NOT APPOINTED
**Executable authorization:** NOT GRANTED

## 1. Purpose

This charter defines the dependency boundary for determining who, if anyone, possesses constitutional authority to exercise an already-established constitutional amendment power.

It does **not** create constitutional authority, appoint an amendment authority holder, grant amendment power, authorize amendment, establish jurisdiction, establish ratification, create constitutional effect, or authorize implementation.

## 2. Dependency on the Source of Amendment Power

Amendment authority is subordinate to the constitutional source of amendment power.

The existence of a claimed authority holder MUST NOT establish the source of amendment power.

The unresolved state therefore remains:

SOURCE OF AMENDMENT POWER
        ↓
AMENDMENT POWER
        ↓
AMENDMENT AUTHORITY

Where the upstream source or power remains unresolved, downstream amendment authority MUST remain unresolved.

## 3. Authority-Holder Determination

Before constitutional amendment authority can be recognized, the constitutional framework MUST establish:

1. who possesses the authority;
2. the constitutional basis for that authority;
3. whether the authority is individual, collective, concurrent, or otherwise constituted;
4. the jurisdiction of the authority;
5. the scope of the authority;
6. the conditions for exercising the authority;
7. the limitations upon the authority;
8. whether the authority may be delegated;
9. whether the authority may be transferred;
10. whether the authority may be suspended or terminated; and
11. what evidence establishes a valid exercise of the authority.

Until these determinations are constitutionally established, the holder remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## 4. Authority Is Not Created by Office or Control

Constitutional amendment authority MUST NOT be inferred merely from:

- administrative office;
- governance role;
- ownership or control of software;
- source-code access;
- database privileges;
- API authority;
- deployment authority;
- authenticated access;
- administrator status;
- operational control;
- organizational seniority;
- appointment by an unverified authority; or
- practical ability to modify the system.

Technical capability MUST NOT be treated as constitutional authority.

## 5. Delegation and Transfer

The constitutional framework MUST independently establish whether amendment authority may be:

- delegated;
- transferred;
- shared;
- exercised concurrently;
- exercised conditionally;
- suspended; or
- terminated.

No delegation or transfer mechanism may create authority greater than that possessed by the authority from which it derives.

A technical delegation mechanism MUST NOT independently establish constitutional delegation.

## 6. Concurrent Authority

Where multiple actors are alleged to possess amendment authority, the constitutional framework MUST determine:

- whether concurrent authority is permitted;
- whether authority is joint or independent;
- whether unanimity, majority consent, or another threshold is required;
- how conflicting exercises of authority are resolved; and
- which authority prevails where jurisdiction overlaps.

Until such rules are established, concurrent amendment authority remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## 7. Self-Amendment Boundary

An authority MUST NOT acquire authority to amend the constitutional provisions defining its own authority merely by exercising existing administrative, technical, or operational control.

Whether self-amendment is constitutionally permitted remains dependent upon the constitutional source of amendment power and separately established amendment rules.

No authority may manufacture its own constitutional amendment jurisdiction by declaration.

## 8. Proposal Does Not Establish Authority

The ability to propose an amendment MUST NOT establish authority to approve, ratify, execute, or give constitutional effect to that amendment.

PROPOSER ≠ AMENDMENT AUTHORITY

unless an already-established constitutional rule expressly provides otherwise.

## 9. Software Boundary

Software MAY represent an amendment-authority state only after the underlying constitutional authority has been independently established.

Software MUST NOT:

- appoint an amendment authority holder;
- transfer constitutional amendment authority;
- delegate constitutional amendment authority;
- expand constitutional amendment authority;
- validate an otherwise defective authority;
- create self-amendment authority; or
- establish constitutional effect.

A database record, role assignment, permission, API capability, workflow state, or successful test MUST NOT itself constitute proof of constitutional amendment authority.

## 10. Fail-Closed Rule

Where the constitutional basis, holder, scope, jurisdiction, delegation rules, transfer rules, limitations, or evidence of amendment authority cannot be established:

AMENDMENT AUTHORITY = UNRESOLVED
AMENDMENT EXECUTION = BLOCKED
CONSTITUTIONAL EFFECT = NOT ESTABLISHED
EXECUTABLE AUTHORIZATION = NOT GRANTED

No governance mechanism may manufacture the missing authority.
