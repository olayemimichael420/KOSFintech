## 4. Non-Creation Rule

This record MUST NOT be interpreted as creating:

- constitutional source;
- amendment power;
- amendment authority;
- jurisdiction;
- mandate;
- external permission;
- approval;
- ratification;
- constitutional effect;
- executable authorization.

Writing, storing, reviewing, approving, committing, deploying, or executing this
document does not by itself constitute constitutional conferral.

## 6. Downstream Preservation

Even if constitutional conferral is validly established:

- the Amendment Authority Charter determines exercise authority;
- the Amendment Procedure Charter determines procedural requirements;
- the Approval/Ratification Charter determines constitutional effectiveness;
- external permissions remain independently required where another jurisdiction is involved;
- software remains an enforcement mechanism and not a constitutional source.

## 7. Anti-Escalation Rule

No holder, administrator, software component, AI system, database state, API privilege,
deployment mechanism, or operational process may enlarge the conferred power beyond its
constitutionally established scope.

Delegated authority cannot exceed the authority from which it is delegated.

## 8. External Permission Boundary

Where a proposed amendment or related action crosses into a jurisdiction not controlled
by the KOS constitutional framework, the Questions-and-Answers service bridge may:

1. identify the applicable external jurisdiction;
2. identify required permission or consent;
3. collect or verify evidence;
4. block execution where required permission is absent.

The bridge MUST NOT manufacture, infer, or self-award external authority.

## 9. Evidence Requirement

A valid conferral determination must identify, at minimum:

1. authoritative source;
2. conferring authority;
3. conferring constitutional act;
4. recipient;
5. power conferred;
6. jurisdiction;
7. scope;
8. limitations;
9. effective event/time;
10. evidence establishing the foregoing.

## 10. Fail-Closed Rule

If any indispensable constitutional element required to establish conferral remains
unresolved:
